Your investments

These amounts show the money you recorded as invested. They do not include live prices, profit/loss or withdrawals. SIP and bhisi payments must be entered each time you pay.

Already investing?

Your daily reminder

Choose a time to check in with your money.

The page reminder works only while this page is open and active. It cannot wake your phone or reliably ring in the background.

Import the downloaded .ics file into your calendar and enable its notifications. Support and sounds depend on your calendar and phone settings. For a ringing alarm, set a daily Clock alarm with the label “Fill Paisa expense tracker”.

Data & backup

Your entries are saved automatically in this browser on this device. They do not sync between phones or browsers. Clearing site data removes them.

Restore replaces current data after confirmation. Excel export includes a summary, all entries, investments and udhari balances. It is a downloaded snapshot, not an automatically updated cloud file.

On your phone, open the hosted website and choose “Add to Home Screen” or “Install app” from the browser menu.
